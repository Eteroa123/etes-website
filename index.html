About

Born and raised in Cannons Creek, Porirua, Eteroa hails from the villages of Fogapoa, Leulumoega Tuai, and Tafagamanu Lefaga in Samoa — where her commitment to service and community leadership first began.

She studied Computer Science and began her career as a Software Engineer, building a strong technical foundation that now underpins her work in emerging technologies. Today she serves as Head of AI and Culture at Fibre Fale, leading initiatives focused on building AI capability while ensuring Pacific values remain at the heart of the global technology conversation.

Her journey into community technology leadership began with DigiTautua — an initiative to refurbish and fundraise devices for families experiencing the digital divide. From that mission, she co-founded Fibre Fale in 2022, a purpose-driven collective creating pathways for Pacific people to confidently enter and lead within the technology sector.

Through Fibre Fale, Eteroa and co-founder Julia Arnott Neenee design and deliver programmes that blend technology, culture, and community — spanning AI education, digital capability building, and spaces where Pacific communities can explore, learn, and lead in emerging technologies.

Eteroa is passionate about ensuring that Pacific communities are not just consumers of technology, but builders, creators, and leaders in shaping the digital future.
